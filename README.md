## Solutions101 — business-automation prototypes

- [s101-autopark](https://github.com/d14life/s101-autopark): equipment-rental fleet system with an occupancy calendar, contracts, shifts, payments and per-machine profitability. [Live demo](https://d14life.github.io/s101-autopark/) (Russian interface)
- [s101-warehouse-terminal](https://github.com/d14life/s101-warehouse-terminal): handheld warehouse-scanner screen and office view with FEFO picking routes, cell and barcode scanning, receiving, putaway, moves and stock counts. [Live demo](https://d14life.github.io/s101-warehouse-terminal/) (Russian interface)
- [s101-accounting](https://github.com/d14life/s101-accounting): counterparty settlements, who owes whom for every customer and supplier, with debt ageing, a payment calendar and a printable reconciliation act. [Live demo](https://d14life.github.io/s101-accounting/) (Russian interface)
- [s101-auto-parts](https://github.com/d14life/s101-auto-parts): auto-parts store where you find parts by VIN or by make and model, filter the catalogue and order through a cart. [Live demo](https://d14life.github.io/s101-auto-parts/) (Russian interface)
- [s101-price-collector](https://github.com/d14life/s101-price-collector): supplier price-list collector, three price lists merge into one table with the lowest price per item. [Live demo](https://d14life.github.io/s101-price-collector/) (Russian interface)
